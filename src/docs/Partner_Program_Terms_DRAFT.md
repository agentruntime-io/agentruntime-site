## 1. WHEN THESE TERMS APPLY

**1.1 Acceptance.** You can earn commission only after AgentRuntime has approved your application and you have accepted these Partner Terms in the Console. We record the version you accepted and when.

**1.2 With Other Terms.** These Partner Terms apply together with the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy, the Acceptable Use Policy and the Billing and Credits Policy. If they conflict on the subject of the partner program, these Partner Terms apply to that subject.

---

## 2. JOINING

**2.1 Applying.** A Console user can apply to become a Partner and describe how they will promote AgentRuntime. AgentRuntime may also invite someone. You do not need a paid workspace to be a Partner.

**2.2 Approval.** AgentRuntime decides whether to approve an application and may reject or end an application or a Partner at its discretion, in line with Section 11. Only an approved Partner can link customers and earn commission.

**2.3 Your Code and Link.** An approved Partner has a referral code and a sign-up link. AgentRuntime may replace the code with a short custom one when it approves you. You cannot change the code yourself.

---

## 3. LINKING A CUSTOMER TO YOU

**3.1 How a Link Is Made.** A customer is linked to you when they use your code or link when they sign up, or, if they have not yet made a payment, when they enter your code in the Billing page of the Console.

**3.2 One Partner Per Customer.** A customer can be linked to only one Partner, and the first code used wins. The link is locked when the customer makes their first payment. Only AgentRuntime can change a link, and it records the reason.

**3.3 No Self-Referral.** A link is not made, and no commission is earned, where the referred workspace was created or is owned by you, where you are its payer, or where its payments are made with a card you also use. Where something else suggests self-referral or abuse, for example you are a member of the referred workspace, the customer shares your non-free email domain, or many sign-ups arrive in a short time, AgentRuntime may hold the commission for review and may release it or cancel it.

**3.4 Welcome Credits.** A customer who uses a Partner's code still receives the welcome credits that any referred friend receives. The Partner receives cash commission under these Partner Terms and not credits.

---

## 4. WHAT EARNS COMMISSION

**4.1 Commission.** You earn commission as a percentage of what a linked customer pays AgentRuntime, calculated on the amount before any value-added tax, sales tax, or similar tax. Top-ups and plan payments, including charges for upgrades, both count.

**4.2 For How Long.** Commission is earned on payments made during a set number of months starting with the customer's first payment.

**4.3 Rate and Months.** The rate and the number of months are set per Partner and are shown in your partner dashboard. They are copied onto each customer's link when the customer makes their first payment. A later change to the rate or to the number of months applies to customers whose first payment comes after the change, and not to customers who are already linked.

**4.4 What Does Not Earn.** No commission is earned on credits that were not paid for (trial, welcome, referral, and support credits), on free or zero-amount invoices, or on failed payments.

---

## 5. HOLD AND PAYABLE

**5.1 Hold.** A commission is held for a period after the payment it comes from, to allow for refunds and disputes. The length of the hold is shown in your partner dashboard. When the hold ends, the commission becomes payable.

**5.2 Disputes.** A commission is not made payable while a dispute on its payment is open.

---

## 6. REFUNDS, DISPUTES, AND MISTAKES

**6.1 Before You Are Paid.** If a customer's payment is refunded in full, the commission for it is cancelled. If it is refunded in part, the commission is reduced in the same proportion. While a dispute on the payment is open, the commission is frozen. If AgentRuntime wins the dispute, it is released; if the customer wins, it is cancelled.

**6.2 After You Are Paid.** If a payment is refunded or lost in a dispute after you were paid the commission for it, that amount is deducted from your next payout and shown to you as an adjustment. AgentRuntime does not take money back from your bank account.

**6.3 Amounts You Owe.** If a deduction is larger than your later earnings, AgentRuntime may carry the difference against your future commission or, at its discretion, write it off.

**6.4 Cancellations.** If a customer cancels, you earn nothing more on that customer. Commission already earned is not changed by the cancellation.

---

## 7. PAYOUTS

**7.1 Schedule and Minimum.** AgentRuntime pays commission once a month, for what is payable at that point, when your payable balance reaches a minimum amount. A smaller balance rolls over to the next month. The minimum and the next payout date are shown in your partner dashboard. AgentRuntime may require its own approval of each payout run.

**7.2 How You Are Paid.** Payouts are made in US dollars, from AgentRuntime's Stripe balance, through Stripe Connect to a payout account that you set up on Stripe's own pages. Stripe checks your identity and holds your bank and tax details. AgentRuntime keeps only your Stripe account identifier and its status. Until your payout account is ready, AgentRuntime may pay you by another method or hold the payout. Stripe supports payouts to some countries only, and AgentRuntime cannot pay a Partner in a country Stripe does not support.

**8.2 Invoices.** If the law requires you to charge value-added tax or to issue an invoice for commission, tell us. AgentRuntime may ask you for an invoice, or may issue a statement on your behalf where the law allows.

**10.2 What We Hold.** AgentRuntime holds your name, email address, program status, referral and commission records, and payout history, and the identifier of your payout account with Stripe, as described in the Privacy Policy. Payout and tax records are kept for as long as the law requires, even if you close your account.
